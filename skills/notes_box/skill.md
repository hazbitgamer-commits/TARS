# notes_box
Opens a small popup window with a text box, since the terminal chat won't let
you paste. Type or paste your text, click "Save & Close" (or press
Ctrl+Enter), and it's saved to `tars/notes/latest_note.txt` for TARS to use.

**Say:** "open a notes box" / "give me somewhere to paste this"
**Args:** `text` — optional; if given, skips the popup and saves this text directly.
