# life_events
Remembers dated personal milestones (first day of school, moving house, first job, etc.) in `events.json` next to this skill, and answers questions about them later by matching keywords and reporting the stored date.
**Say:** "my first day at primary school was 1st of February 2016 at Orbin Grove Primary School" / "when was my first day of school"
**Args:** `text`.
