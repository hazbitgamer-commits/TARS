# speakers
Google Nest control over local Cast: announce/volume/pause/resume/stop/list. Devices cached in speakers_cache.json. Announcements via Google TTS URL (speaker fetches it directly).
**Say:** "announce dinner is ready in the kitchen" / "set the bedroom speaker to 30" / "pause the kitchen display"
**Args:** `action`; `room` — kitchen/bedroom/all; `text`; `level`.
